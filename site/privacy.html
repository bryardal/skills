Privacy

Your email is used to send you the skills and updates you signed up for. Nothing else.

What we collect

Your email address and which skill page you signed up from. Signups go through a bot check by Cloudflare Turnstile.

Where it's kept

Emails are stored with our email provider, Resend, and used only to send messages from Bry Ardal. They are never sold or shared.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted, reply to any email and ask.

On this site

The site remembers that you signed up, in your browser, so it doesn't ask again. There are no ads or tracking scripts.
