Privacy

We only use your email to send you the skills and updates you signed up for.

What we keep

We keep your email address and the skill page you signed up on. Cloudflare Turnstile checks each signup to stop bots.

Where it's kept

Your email is stored with our email provider, Resend. We use it only to send emails from Bry Ardal, and we never sell or share it.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted, reply to any email and ask.

On this site

Your browser remembers that you signed up, so the site doesn't ask again. The site has no ads and no tracking scripts.
