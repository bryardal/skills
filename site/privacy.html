Privacy

Your email is used to send you the skills and updates you signed up for. Nothing else.

What's collected

Your email address and which skill page you signed up on. Signups pass a bot check by Cloudflare Turnstile.

Where it's kept

With the email provider, Resend, used only to send emails from Bry Ardal. It's never sold or shared.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted, reply to any email and ask.

On this site

Your browser remembers that you signed up, so the site doesn't ask again. There are no ads or tracking scripts.
