# v2 stress test: 24 Oct 2026 (skill 2.1)

10 machine and living-thing lessons the skill had never seen, each built by its own agent from SKILL.md alone, in
parallel, on one shared machine (load average 35-55 for most of the run, so checks were slow). None of the builders
could start helper agents, so every fact check was self-run (accuracy capped at 4). After the fixes below, all 10
pass `check.py` with the final engine. One fresh reviewer scored all 10 blind (codes L01-L10, random order, no
knowledge of who built them or how).

## Findings and fixes (all shipped in 2.1 unless noted)

| Problem | Seen in | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Parts builders had to hand-write: chain or belt, slope and force arrows, a limb, a protein pocket, base letters, a hinged flap, an air sac | 8 of 10 builders | `belt`, `incline`, `force`, `arm`, `enzyme`, `helix({seq, letters})`, `flap`, `chain`, `alveoli`, `pad`, `pulse`, `upTo`, `bloodColour`, `rbc` tokens; all in parts-gallery |
| Heavy pages (gears about 125 elements each, 7-17 s to open under load) | 02, 04, 10 | gear walls joined by colour (125 to about 11 elements), latheAx faces joined, organs cached, element budget in api.md, check.py names the busiest view |
| check.py crashed on a slow machine (30 s page timeout) | 5 builders | long timeouts; a PROBLEM line instead of a crash |
| Covered press target reported without saying what covers it | 09 | the PROBLEM names the covering part or shape and the fix |
| The pulsing ring could land beside a thin part or under a label | 09 | the ring moves to the nearest point a tap really reaches |
| Poster: tiny picture, the start moment with arrows missing, steps list cut off | 02, 09 | picture cropped to what is drawn, full-width layout for wide pictures, steps under the picture, text shrinks until it fits, print.py reports anything still cut and the labels at each moment |
| Diagram: tall picture overflowed, legends crowded the small step pictures | 01 | picture fits its column; no legend in the strip |

## Turning (item 6 of the request)

Tested on molecule shapes (methane, ammonia, water): turning shows the side-on bend of water and the pyramid of
ammonia, which a fixed view hides. Kept, documented for 3D shapes that are themselves the idea; not for machines or
organs (flat textbook sections read better).

## Video and print

Video download tested on the kettle (8.1 MB) and the candle with its flame effect (13.6 MB, 130 s); headless it takes
4-8 minutes, so check.py now waits up to 20. Posters, diagrams and handouts printed for heart, bike gears, DNA and
molecule shapes: each fits its page.
