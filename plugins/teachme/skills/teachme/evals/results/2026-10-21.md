## Findings, most common first

| Problem | Lessons | Fixed in 1.12 |
|---|---|---|
| No sub-agent tool, so no independent fact check and no background research | 12 | Fallback in SKILL.md and research.md: budgeted research and a cold fact check; hand-off says it was self-run |
| Labels piled at the frame edge, labels for parts out of view, legend lost or covering labels when zoomed | 7 | Labels hide when their part is out of view, step aside from each other, the legend and the page controls; legend 'auto' |
| api.md missing argument types (`shade` must be a function: crashed 3 lessons; `byDepth`, `fill` extra, `tx` anchor, `ground`, toggles, do + guess) | 8 | Engine accepts any `shade`; api.md documents every argument |
| No see-through / fade, rings and ripples, level meters, glass vessels, coloured or dashed lines, any-outline solids, custom token shapes | 2-5 each | `fade`, `ring`, `ripples`, `meter`, `vessel`, `curve`, `prism`, `TOKENS.x.draw`, `ion+`/`ion-` |
| The isometric view distorts what the topic is about (a proof's squares, a cross-section, a chart) | 4 | `view: 'front'` and `view: 'plan'` |
| No guidance for abstract or human topics (feelings, institutions, debated history, mixed scales) | 5 | "Any topic" pattern table in drawing.md; `views` for debated topics; illustrative-model rule; people as pegs |
| Rules that didn't fit some topics (plain steps, looping end states, moving parts, label limit, zoom range, word limits) | 6 | Clarified in design.md and api.md |
| WebFetch paraphrases, so quotes may not be word for word | 5 | Fetch prompts ask for exact sentences; unconfirmed quotes are marked. Still open: a raw-page check |
| `check.py --step` re-ran the whole check (80 s); legend problems not caught | 3 | `--step N` alone is a 3-second single view; legends are checked |
| Auto-framing counted clipped, invisible drawing | 1 | Ignored now |
| A sound topic had no sound | 1 | `playNote` and a Sound switch in Options |
