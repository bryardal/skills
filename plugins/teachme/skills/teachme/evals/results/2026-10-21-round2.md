## Findings, most common first (all fixed in the final 1.13 unless noted)

| Problem | Lessons | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No example used the new parts; sizes and units guessed | 8 of 12 builders | 4 new examples; api.md names the example for each part |
| Auto zoom cropped the wrong thing; switched off | 6 builders, 9 reviewed | Frames everything that changes; keeps cards and charts whole; room for readouts; max 1.8x |
| Wrong argument types blanked the drawing, no location | 7 | '#hex' everywhere, dash strings, safe fallbacks; check.py names the element; syntax errors reported |
| Steps only watched or only guessed | 12 reviewed | Rule: every step but one has a do or drag; check.py lists them |
| Text in cards, bubbles and charts too small | 10 reviewed | Size multiplier, bigger defaults, larger chart text |
| Abstract steps carried by a glow or bubble | 8 reviewed | Rule: show the consequence; bubbles 2-6 words |
| Coverage warning pushed the wrong fix | 7 | Warns only below 10%, says when that's fine |
| Boxy furniture; no sitting or holding | 5 reviewed | `table`; `person` sit and hold; `personHand` |
| Inset collided in front view; skewed close-ups | 3 | `FAR` default; `view` and `marker` options |
