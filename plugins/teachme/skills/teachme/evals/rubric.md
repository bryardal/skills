# Scoring rubric (1-5 each)

Score from the contact sheet, a click-through of the built lesson, and its fact sheet. Be strict: 3 is "works,
with clear flaws".

| Score | Accuracy | Picture clarity | Hands-on | Writing | Visual polish | Real detail | Fit of the skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Every line traced to a quote; independent check, 0 weak left | A stranger names what happens in every step from the picture alone | Every step is something the learner causes; the sandbox tests the big idea | Plain, short, one word per thing, no filler | Looks like a finished illustration at every size | Built from its real parts; zoomed in, every region has something true to find, nothing invented | The engine had everything; no workarounds |
| 4 | Traced; self-run check, or 1-2 soft spots | One step needs its caption | Most steps act; sandbox good | One or two long or stiff lines | Small blemishes (a crowded corner, a small part) | Real and recognisable; one or two plain regions | One or two small workarounds |
| 3 | Traced but some sources weak or paraphrased | Several steps need the caption | Acting is token (press to continue) in places | Some jargon or long sentences | Boxy, crowded or small in places | Recognisable at 1x but bare when zoomed | Real workarounds (30+ lines) |
| 2 | Gaps; some lines not traceable | The picture mostly decorates the text | Mostly watching | Hard to follow | Unfinished look | A few slabs or blobs standing for the thing | The format fought the topic |
| 1 | Wrong facts | Misleading picture | None | Wrong level | Broken | Invented or wrong parts | Couldn't be done |

Real detail applies to `kind: 'machine'` and `'living'`; score idea lessons n/a. Living lessons also need a stated
scale and, for health topics, the notice (a missing one caps Accuracy at 3).

Also record: minutes, tool calls, `check.py` runs, the visual pattern used (drawing.md "Any topic"), and the top 3
problems in the builder's words.
