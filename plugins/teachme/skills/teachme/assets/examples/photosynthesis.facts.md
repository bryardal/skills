# Fact sheet: photosynthesis (student level)

Checked 21 Oct 2026. Every line in the lesson maps to a fact below.

## The real process

1. Light reaches the leaves. Chloroplasts in leaf cells hold chlorophyll, which absorbs the light's energy.
2. Roots take in water from the soil; xylem in the stem carries it up to the leaves.
3. Carbon dioxide from the air enters the leaf through stomata.
4. Using light energy, water is split (oxidized); the oxygen leaves through the stomata into the air.
5. The energy is used to build sugar (glucose) from carbon dioxide.
6. Phloem carries sugar from the leaves to other parts such as the roots; it is used for energy or stored as starch.
7. Most of the plant's mass comes from carbon dioxide taken from the air; very little comes from the soil.

## Common mistakes (used as wrong answers)

- The oxygen comes from carbon dioxide (it comes from water: fact 8).
- Plants get their mass from the soil (mostly from carbon dioxide in the air: fact 10).
- More light always means faster sugar-making (it levels off when something else limits it: fact 12).

## What-ifs for Try it yourself

- No light: no sugar is made (fact 11).
- More light speeds it up until carbon dioxide (or temperature) limits it (fact 12).
- More carbon dioxide raises the limit (fact 12).
